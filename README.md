# Trading-Volatility




This project is involved in explaining and testing some of the trading strategies listed in (Z. Kakushadze and J.A. Serur. 151 Trading Strategies). I have always been interested in the idea of non directional trading, having zero exposure to the underlying. The strategies I focus on here involve trading volaltity, this means taking a position on future volatility,

the strategies I address are:
- Volatility trading with variance swaps
- Volatility risk premium trading, gamma hedging
