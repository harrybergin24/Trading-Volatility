# Trading Volatility 

This project contains 3 sub-projects, all aiming at investigating volatility from different angels. The first sub-project looks at a method to try and trade volatility, by extracting the Volatility Risk Premium. This looks at a more trading and systematic strategy view looking to try and generate returns from a specific market behavior. The second sub-project looks at trading derivatives on volatility, I am currently finalising a variance swap pricer which quotes variance swaps in volatility rather than variance. I am working on attempting to make this part interactive as possible. The final part is trying to predict volatility, I found when trying to apply the strategy created in the first part of the project Applying a GARCH model to every asset was not accurate due to the different distribution of realised volatility, instead of going through and fitting a different types of GARCH models I am learning more machine learning to create a machine learning model that can predict volatility and 'vol of vol', the latter being crucial for pricing volatility derivatives. All of these projects are working projects and are constantly being updated.
